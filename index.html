Privacy-first by design

FraudShield’s concept is to reduce unnecessary exposure of transaction identifiers while supporting risk analysis.

  • Masked labels in list views
  • Demo uses synthetic records
  • Reminder to avoid sensitive inputs
  • Privacy controls are demonstrative
This prototype does not implement formal differential privacy or production-grade access security.

Data preparation

Planned pipeline includes data cleaning, categorical label encoding, numerical scaling with StandardScaler and train/test separation.

⤧

Imbalance handling

SMOTE is planned to synthesize minority-class samples for the training workflow, if included in the final training pipeline.

◈

Privacy safeguards

Masked presentation, synthetic examples and careful data access are intended to limit sensitive data exposure. The DNN alone does not guarantee privacy.

The project plan mentions a large financial dataset, preprocessing, SMOTE, a DNN and a Flask backend. The running browser demo is a simplified synthetic model and does not claim those training steps are currently active.
